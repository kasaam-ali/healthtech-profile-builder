title: Appointment Booking Policy
---
Northwind Wellness Clinic offers wellness coaching appointments Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00 clinic time. Saturday and Sunday are closed.

A member may place a temporary hold on one open slot. A hold is a request only and lasts 24 hours until staff confirm it. A member can have at most 2 active holds at a time.

Holds require the member to be logged in and to tick an explicit confirmation box. Holds can be released by the member at any time.
