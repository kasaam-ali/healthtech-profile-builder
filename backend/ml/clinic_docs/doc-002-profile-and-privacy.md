title: Profile Data and Privacy Policy
---
Members can view, update, and delete only their own profile sections: demographics, wellness preferences, and emergency contacts. Staff cannot edit member profiles through the member app.

A member can store up to 5 emergency contacts. Each contact needs a name, a relationship, and a phone number.

Profile data is fictional demo data in this project. The clinic never sells profile data. Audit logs record who changed which section and when, but never store passwords, tokens, or full profile values.
