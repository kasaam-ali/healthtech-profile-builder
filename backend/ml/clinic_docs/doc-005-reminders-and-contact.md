title: Reminders and Contact Channels Policy
---
Members can opt in to reminders by email, SMS, or in-app notification. Reminders are sent 24 hours before an appointment. Members can turn reminders off in wellness preferences.

The clinic support desk answers non-medical account questions by email at support@northwind.example within 2 business days. The support desk does not give medical advice.
